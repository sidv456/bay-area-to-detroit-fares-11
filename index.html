Before we decide

Zipjet is cheapest, but bags cost extra

Option 3 is $214 each, and the $428 total does not cover bags. Add up the bag fees for both of us before comparing it with the others. It also gets us home Sunday evening, not Monday morning.

Options 1 and 4 could use our miles

Both SkyBridge flights are marked as bookable with SkyBridge miles. The email does not say how many miles they cost, so check on SkyBridge before counting on it.

Same SkyBridge flight home, one day apart going

Options 1 and 4 both return Monday Nov 16 at 8:15am. Leaving Friday instead of Thursday costs $43 more each ($86 for both) and gives us one less night in Detroit.
